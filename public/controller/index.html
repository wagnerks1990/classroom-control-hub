Morning Announcements • Live Watch
On student school days, RoomGoblin watches the Ant Media stream during the morning window. When it becomes live, it takes all configured targets fullscreen with audio-on redundancy. When the stream ends, it clears the announcement and releases the displays back to the normal scheduler.

      Loading…
    

    

      
      
      
      
      
    

    

    
Targets: all enabled displays. Two consecutive offline checks are required before an active announcement is ended, preventing a brief stream hiccup from blanking the TVs.

How schedules work

    

      RoomGoblin checks the schedule every 15 seconds. Events can run on normal weekly weekdays, configured rotating school days,
      or exact calendar dates. District no-school dates above are globally suppressed. The configured rotation counts
      only eligible Monday–Friday school days, so blocked dates do not advance the rotation. Schedule and calendar settings are saved in the RoomGoblin database.
